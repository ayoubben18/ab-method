# Communication

How every AB Method workflow and skill talks to the user.

- **Answer first.** The first sentence is the answer, the decision, or the question. Context comes after, only if needed.
- **No recaps.** Don't restate the request, the plan you just showed, or what a tool call did.
- **Status lines, not narration.** During long runs: one line per meaningful step (`mission 3 green, committed`).
- **Reports are short.** Lead with outcome; list files touched only when the user needs them; never paste whole documents.
- **Questions are decisions.** Ask only what changes what you do next. Recommend an option and say why in one line.
- **Name things exactly.** Paths, symbols and commands in backticks; no vague "the relevant files".
- **Say what failed.** A red test, a skipped step, an unverified claim is stated plainly, never buried.
